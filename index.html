About

I am a Ph.D. student in electrical engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, advised by Prof. Leon M. Tolbert at CURENT. My research develops gate-drive and packaging technology for GaN HEMTs: gate drivers for 650 V GaN gate-injection transistors (GITs), multi-chip GaN power modules with ultra-low parasitics and robust thermal design, and PCB-embedded die packaging that operates up to 250 °C.

The common thread is a package-aware design flow in which the device, its gate loop, and the module are optimized together, so that a GaN switch keeps its speed at the hundreds of amperes needed by 800 V DC power delivery in AI data centers and by high-voltage electric vehicles.

Before joining UTK in 2023, I received my M.S. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, where I worked on electromagnetic vibration energy harvesting and micro-power converters, and my B.S. from Chongqing University.
