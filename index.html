About

I am a Ph.D. student in electrical engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, advised by Prof. Leon M. Tolbert at CURENT. My research develops gate-drive and packaging technology for GaN HEMTs: gate drivers for 650 V GaN Schottky-gate (SG) transistors and gate-injection transistors (GITs), and multi-chip GaN power modules with ultra-low parasitics and a robust assembly process for high-current applications.

My dissertation, Switching Modeling, Driver Technology and Packaging of GaN HEMTs in High-Current Applications, treats the device, its gate loop, and the module as one design problem, so that a GaN switch keeps its speed at the hundreds of amperes needed by electric vehicles and by 800 V DC power delivery in AI data centers. The proposal defense is expected in November 2026.

Before joining UTK in 2023, I received my M.S. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, where I worked on electromagnetic vibration energy harvesting and micro-power converters, and my B.S. from Chongqing University.
